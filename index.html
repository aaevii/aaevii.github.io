aevi

INFORMATION
aevi is a digital artist portfolio exploring fragmented memory, analog distortion, visual decay, and experimental motion.

Inspired by underground broadcast systems, VHS texture, CRT artifacts, and anti-clean digital aesthetics.

The work exists between signal and collapse.
